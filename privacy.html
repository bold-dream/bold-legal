At Bold, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

Profile Information

  • Name, age, photos, bio, and preferences
  • Location data (with your permission)
  • Device information and usage patterns

Communication Data

  • Messages between users
  • Match and interaction history
  • Customer support communications

Payment Information

  • Subscription and purchase history
  • Payment method details (processed securely)

How We Use Your Information

To Provide Our Service

  • Create and maintain your profile
  • Show you potential matches
  • Enable messaging and communication
  • Process subscriptions and payments

To Improve Our Service

  • Analyze usage patterns and preferences
  • Develop new features and improvements
  • Conduct research and analytics

For Safety and Security

  • Verify identity and prevent fraud
  • Investigate violations and abuse
  • Ensure platform safety and integrity

Information Sharing

We don't sell your personal information. We may share data with:

Service Providers

  • Technology partners who help operate our service
  • Payment processors for subscription handling
  • Analytics providers for service improvement

Legal Requirements

  • When required by law or court order
  • To protect rights, safety, or property
  • In connection with business transactions

Your Privacy Rights

You can:

  • Access and download your data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and data
  • Control who can see your profile
  • Opt out of marketing communications

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encryption of sensitive data
  • Secure data transmission protocols
  • Regular security assessments
  • Limited access to personal information

Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as necessary to:

  • Provide our services
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes and enforce agreements

International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries with different privacy laws. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

Children's Privacy

Bold is not intended for users under 18. We don't knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this policy. We'll notify you of significant changes via the app or email.
