Children's Privacy

Bold is not intended for users under 18. We don't knowingly collect information from minors.

Zero-Tolerance Child Safety Policy

Bold Dating maintains a strict, zero-tolerance policy against any form of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).

Users are strictly prohibited from creating, uploading, sharing, requesting, or distributing any content that exploits, harms, or threatens minors. Any account found engaging in, facilitating, or promoting CSAE or CSAM will be permanently banned immediately, and a report will be filed with the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and appropriate law enforcement authorities.

Reporting Mechanisms & CSAM Removal

We provide in-app tools allowing users to instantly report any violative content or behavior. If you encounter any content that violates our Child Safety Standards, please use the in-app reporting button next to the post or profile, or contact us immediately via our dedicated safety channel below.

Our moderation team reviews reported items 24/7. Any content identified as CSAM or CSAE will be removed immediately upon discovery.

Compliance and Legal Standards

Bold Dating complies with all applicable local, national, and international child safety laws and regulations. We actively cooperate with regulatory bodies and law enforcement to ensure our platform remains safe.
