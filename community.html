Community Guidelines

Bold is a community built on respect, authenticity, and meaningful connections. These guidelines help create a safe and positive environment for everyone.

Be Authentic

  • Use recent, accurate photos of yourself
  • Provide truthful information in your profile
  • Don't impersonate others or create fake accounts
  • Be genuine in your interactions

Respect Others

  • Treat everyone with kindness and respect
  • Respect boundaries and consent
  • No harassment, bullying, or abusive behavior
  • Accept rejection gracefully

Stay Safe

  • Protect your personal information
  • Meet in public places for first dates
  • Trust your instincts about people and situations
  • Report suspicious or concerning behavior

Keep It Appropriate

  • No nudity, sexually explicit content, or suggestive poses
  • No hate speech, discriminatory language, or offensive content
  • No violence, weapons, or illegal activities
  • Keep conversations respectful and appropriate

Communication Standards

  • Be respectful in your messages
  • No spam, promotional content, or commercial solicitation
  • Don't send unsolicited explicit messages
  • Engage in genuine conversation

Photo Guidelines

  • Photos must clearly show your face
  • No group photos where you can't be identified
  • No photos of children (even if they're yours)
  • No copyrighted images or photos of others

What We Don't Allow

Zero-Tolerance Child Safety Policy

Bold Dating maintains a strict, zero-tolerance policy against any form of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).

Users are strictly prohibited from creating, uploading, sharing, requesting, or distributing any content that exploits, harms, or threatens minors. Any account found engaging in, facilitating, or promoting CSAE or CSAM will be permanently banned immediately, and a report will be filed with the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and appropriate law enforcement authorities.

Reporting Mechanisms & CSAM Removal

We provide in-app tools allowing users to instantly report any violative content or behavior. If you encounter any content that violates our Child Safety Standards, please use the in-app reporting button next to the post or profile, or contact us immediately via our dedicated safety channel below.

Our moderation team reviews reported items 24/7. Any content identified as CSAM or CSAE will be removed immediately upon discovery.

Compliance and Legal Standards

Bold Dating complies with all applicable local, national, and international child safety laws and regulations. We actively cooperate with regulatory bodies and law enforcement to ensure our platform remains safe.

Dedicated Child Safety Point of Contact

If you have any questions about these standards, or if you need to flag an urgent matter regarding minor safety, please contact our dedicated Child Safety Officer:

  • Dedicated Safety Email: safety@boldapp.com
  • Response Window: Monitored continuously for urgent safety escalations.

Prohibited Behavior

  • Harassment, stalking, or threatening behavior
  • Sharing intimate images without consent
  • Soliciting money or financial information
  • Promoting illegal activities or substances
  • Creating multiple accounts to evade bans

Prohibited Content

  • Graphic violence or disturbing imagery
  • Hate symbols or extremist content
  • Spam, scams, or fraudulent schemes
  • Content promoting self-harm

Reporting and Enforcement

If you encounter violations:

  • Report inappropriate profiles or behavior immediately
  • Block users who make you uncomfortable
  • We investigate all reports promptly
  • Violations may result in warnings, suspensions, or permanent bans

Age Verification

All users must be at least 18 years old. We may require age verification for certain features or if accounts are flagged.

Privacy and Consent

  • Always respect others' privacy and boundaries
  • Don't share private conversations or photos without permission
  • Obtain clear consent before meeting in person
  • Don't pressure others into uncomfortable situations

Creating Positive Connections

  • Be open to different types of people and conversations
  • Focus on genuine connection over superficial judgments
  • Practice good communication and active listening
  • Be patient and understanding with others

Consequences

Violations of these guidelines may result in:

  • Warning messages
  • Temporary account restrictions
  • Permanent account suspension
  • Removal from the platform

We reserve the right to investigate and take appropriate action against any behavior that violates these guidelines or threatens the safety and well-being of our community.

Updates

These guidelines may be updated periodically. Continued use of Bold indicates acceptance of any changes.

Questions

For questions about these guidelines, contact us at community@boldapp.com.

Remember: Bold is about building meaningful connections. By following these guidelines, you help create a positive experience for everyone in our community.
